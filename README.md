# Royal Medical Disclosures

Create ONLY the “FDA & Compounding Disclaimers” section for the Royal Medical Center homepage.

This is a completely new Lovable chat. Do not assume any previous conversation, design, components, or implementation.

Build this as a standalone, production-ready section that can be placed near the lower portion of the homepage, before the final CTA/footer.

IMPORTANT

This is a medical/legal disclaimer section.

Do NOT rewrite, simplify, reinterpret, strengthen, or add claims to the disclaimer content.

Use the disclaimer content below exactly as provided.

Do not invent additional legal language.

SECTION TITLE

Use exactly:

FDA & Compounding Disclaimers

DISCLAIMER CONTENT

Display the following four disclaimer points exactly:

1.

The FDA does not verify the safety, effectiveness, or quality of compounded drugs offered at our clinic.

2.

As an alternative to FDA-approved branded products, where appropriate, a provider may prescribe a compounded drug, which is prepared by a state-licensed sterile compounding pharmacy partner. Although compounded drugs are permitted to be prescribed under federal law, they are not FDA-approved and do not undergo safety, effectiveness, or manufacturing review.

3.

Claims of personalization and potential reduction in side effects are based on the ability of compounding to customize treatment for individual needs. These benefits are not guaranteed. Side effects may still occur and vary by patient.

4.

Compounded medications offered through this service are prescribed on an individual basis by licensed healthcare providers. While some patients may experience weight loss or other benefits, results are not guaranteed. Outcomes depend on a variety of factors including, but not limited to, patient health status, genetics, lifestyle, diet, and exercise. Neither the prescribing provider nor the pharmacy makes any promise or warranty of specific results.

IMPORTANT CONTENT CORRECTION

The current RMC website contains a truncated/incomplete sentence in disclaimer #4.

For this new design, use the grammatically complete wording above:

“Compounded medications offered through this service are prescribed on an individual basis by licensed healthcare providers.”

Do not reproduce the broken website text:

“licensed healthcare While some patients…”

The purpose is only to restore the obvious missing word “providers” so the sentence is readable.

Do not otherwise alter the meaning.
